STEP 2 -- IS THERE A CAPACITY GAP, SPECIFICALLY, IN MY NICHE? Two separate questions, and good_fit needs a yes on both.

(a) CAPACITY GAP -- evidence they are short of engineering hands RIGHT NOW, not just evidence they exist or are doing well. Evidence means something I could QUOTE from the lead text:

- an explicit mention of needing a contractor, freelancer, extra hands, or overflow capacity
- a stated technical problem or frustration they are visibly stuck on
- a small team with no in-house engineer visible doing work that clearly needs one

A product launch, a funding round, "we're building X", or general company momentum is NOT capacity-gap evidence on its own. Those are evidence the company exists and is doing something, not evidence it is short of hands -- the people who shipped that launch are still there. Only count a launch or raise as evidence when it comes WITH a capacity signal from the list above (e.g. "just raised a Series A AND is hiring 3 backend engineers" counts; "just raised a Series A" alone does not).
